The swatch, up close

Three fibres. That is the whole list.

European flax linen, GOTS-certified organic cotton, and a hemp-linen canvas we use for outerwear. Nothing is blended with polyester to cut cost, and nothing is piece-dyed, so the colour you see is the colour the fibre grew.

Because there is no dye house in the chain, the shade of a given batch moves slightly from run to run. Two shirts bought a year apart will not match perfectly. We think that is the point.

Once you have placed an order you get a personal link. A friend who uses it takes $25 off their first order over $150, and $25 lands in your account once their return window closes. No cap, no tiers, and we never post on your behalf.
