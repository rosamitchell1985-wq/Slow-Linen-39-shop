How to read the sizing on this page

Every card lists the model's height and the size they are wearing, because a garment measurement means little on its own. Our fits run from close (the merino rib) to genuinely oversized (the Weaver's Overshirt), and we say which is which rather than calling everything a regular fit.

US letter sizes run XS to XXL. The size guide converts those to US numeric sizes and gives body and garment measurements in inches.
