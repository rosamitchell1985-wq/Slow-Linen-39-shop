Disclaimer and affiliate disclosure

Effective August 27, 2026 Last updated August 27, 2026

This page is a starting template and should be reviewed by an attorney before going live. It supplements the terms of service rather than replacing anything in them.

1. General information disclaimer

Everything on this site is published in good faith and for general information about our own products. We take reasonable care to keep it accurate and current. We do not warrant that it is complete, error free, or suitable for any particular purpose, and we are not liable for any loss arising from reliance on it, except as set out in the terms of service or as required by law.

2. Colour and photography

Screens differ. Colour on your display will not match cloth in daylight, and we cannot control your monitor's calibration, your operating system's colour profile, or the ambient light in your room.

This matters more for us than for most sellers, because our fabric is undyed. Undyed linen, cotton and wool vary from batch to batch by roughly half a tone by design, since there is no dye step to standardise the colour. Two pieces bought a year apart will not match exactly. We say so on every product page, and we would rather you order pieces you intend to wear together in a single order.

If colour is the reason a piece does not work for you, that is a valid reason to return it within 30 days under the normal returns policy.

3. Measurements and sizing

Garment measurements published in the size guide and on product pages are taken by hand from production samples and are accurate to within about half an inch. Natural fibre cloth relaxes and recovers, so a garment measured cold on a table and the same garment measured after a day of wear will differ slightly.

Fit advice we give by email is our opinion based on the measurements you send us. It is a recommendation, not a guarantee, and it does not affect your right to return or exchange.

4. Materials, care and durability claims

Fibre contents and origins come from our suppliers' documentation and from certificates we hold on file, which we will show you on request. Care instructions come from our own laundering tests on production fabric.

Statements about how long a garment lasts are our expectations based on seven years of selling and repairing our own clothes. They are not promises. What is a promise is the five-year seam warranty in the terms of service, which is specific and enforceable.

On the sustainability page we separate figures we measure ourselves from figures we estimate from a named published source, and we identify the parts of the supply chain where we do not have data. Anything labelled as an estimate should be read as order-of-magnitude, not precision.

5. External links

This site links to third-party websites, including opt-out pages operated by Google and by industry bodies, and to the websites of our stockists. We do not control those sites, we are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices, and a link is not an endorsement of everything on the destination site.

6. Advertising disclosure

This site contains clearly labelled advertising placements. Advertisements are marked Advertisement, are visually distinct from our own editorial and product content, and are never placed above the main content of a page, inside navigation, or on legal pages or the error page.

Advertising is served by third-party networks, which may include Google. No advertising script loads unless you have allowed the advertising cookie category. What each vendor sets is listed in the cookie policy.

We do not control which specific advertisements appear. An advertisement on this site is not an endorsement by Slow Linen Organic of the advertiser, its products or its claims. If you see an advertisement here that is misleading or offensive, tell us at hello@slowlinenorganic.com and we will block that advertiser.

7. Affiliate disclosure

In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising, 16 CFR Part 255, we disclose the following.

This site currently contains no affiliate links. We earn nothing when you click a link to a stockist, to a certification body, or to any other external site. Our stockist list is not paid placement; those eleven shops buy our goods at wholesale and appear on that page because they carry the range.

If that ever changes, we will do all of the following:

  • Update this page and change the date at the top.
  • Disclose the relationship at the point of use, immediately next to the link, not only here.
  • Use plain wording such as "we may earn a commission if you buy through this link".
  • Keep the disclosure visible without expanding anything, hovering, or scrolling past it.
  • Never let a commission decide the order of a recommendation.

Similarly, we do not currently accept payment for editorial coverage, we do not publish sponsored posts, and no product mentioned in our journal was gifted to us in exchange for coverage.

8. Testimonials and reviews

We do not publish customer reviews or testimonials anywhere on this site. Nothing on these pages is a customer quotation. If we add a review system in future it will show unedited reviews, including negative ones, or it will not exist.

The names appearing in our lookbook credits are our photographer, producer and models, who were engaged and paid for that work. They are not endorsements.

9. No professional advice

Nothing on this site is legal, financial, medical or professional advice. The cost-per-wear arithmetic in our journal is illustrative and is not financial advice. Statements about fibre properties are drawn from general textile science and our own testing, not from clinical research, and are not advice about skin sensitivity or allergy.

10. Forward-looking statements

Where we describe something we intend to do, such as opening international shipping or improving packaging, that is a current intention and not a commitment. Dates we publish for limited runs are firm commitments and we treat them as such.

11. Contact

Questions about anything on this page go to hello@slowlinenorganic.com, or write to Slow Linen Organic LLC, 418 Alder Mill Road, Suite 200, Asheville, NC 28801, United States.
