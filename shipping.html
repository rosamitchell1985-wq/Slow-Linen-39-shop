What shipping costs, and how long it actually takes.

Free within the United States on orders over $150. A flat $8 below that, however many items are in the order. We ship from Asheville, North Carolina, and we do not ship internationally yet.

How long before it leaves us

In-stock items

Packed and handed to the carrier within two business days. Orders placed after 2pm Eastern count as the next business day. We do not dispatch on weekends or federal holidays.

Made-to-order items

Eighteen to twenty-four business days from payment, then the transit time on top. We email you the week your piece goes on the cutting table.

Mixed orders

If your order has both, we ship the in-stock part immediately and the made-to-order part when it is ready. There is no extra charge for the second parcel.

Tracking, packaging and delivery

Tracking

You get a tracking number by email the moment the label is scanned by the carrier, not when we print it. If you have no tracking email 48 hours after ordering, check your spam folder and then write to hello@slowlinenorganic.com with your order number.

Packaging

Orders ship in an unbleached kraft paper mailer sealed with paper tape. There is no plastic polybag around the garment, no tissue and no branded box. The mailer is curbside recyclable in most US municipalities. The only plastic in the parcel is the face sheet of the shipping label, which we have not found a workable alternative to at our volume.

Signature and safe drop

Nothing requires a signature by default. If your order is over $400 the carrier may request one at their discretion. You can add a delivery instruction at checkout and we pass it to the carrier, though whether they honour it is genuinely out of our hands.

Address changes

We can change an address before dispatch. Email as soon as you can with the order number. Once the label is scanned the address is fixed and the parcel has to complete its journey before it can be redirected.

Seasonal cut-off dates

  • December 15, 2026: last day for standard shipping to arrive by December 24 in the contiguous US
  • December 19, 2026: last day for expedited two-day
  • December 22, 2026: last day for overnight, ordered before 11am Eastern
  • November 20, 2026: last day to order a made-to-order piece for December delivery
  • December 8, 2026: last day for Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico

Gift cards are delivered by email on the date you choose and have no cut-off. How gift cards work.

If something goes wrong

Give it 24 hours first. Carriers occasionally scan a parcel as delivered a day before it arrives, and parcels are often left with a neighbour or in a spot you would not check. After 24 hours, write to us with the order number. We open a carrier trace and, if it does not resolve within seven business days, we reship or refund at your choice. You are not expected to chase the carrier yourself.

Photograph the outer mailer before you open it if you can, then photograph the contents. Email both to hello@slowlinenorganic.com. We replace damaged goods immediately and deal with the carrier ourselves afterwards.

It happens roughly once in every four hundred orders and it is our error, so it costs you nothing. We email a prepaid label, and we ship the correct item as soon as you tell us rather than waiting for the wrong one to come back.

Delivery estimates are estimates and weather, holidays and carrier backlogs all affect them. If a standard order has not moved for five business days, write to us and we will chase it. If an expedited or overnight order misses its guaranteed date because of the carrier, we refund the shipping difference without being asked.

Thirty days from delivery, worn or unworn. Exchanges and store credit ship back to us free; refunds to the original payment method have $7 deducted for the label. The returns page walks through it step by step.
