Privacy policy

Effective August 27, 2026 Last updated August 27, 2026

This policy is written to be read. Where a term has a legal meaning we have used it and then explained it in plain language. If anything here is unclear, email privacy@slowlinenorganic.com and a person will answer.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Order information: name, billing address, shipping address, email address, telephone number if you provide one, and what you ordered.
  • Payment information: handled by our payment processor. We receive the last four digits of the card, the card brand and an authorisation token. We never receive or store the full card number or the security code.
  • Account information: if you create an account, your email address, a hashed password and your order history.
  • Correspondence: the content of emails, contact form submissions and wholesale enquiries you send us, and our replies.
  • Marketing preferences: your email address if you subscribe to drop notices, and whether you have unsubscribed.
  • Measurements: if you send us body measurements for sizing advice, we keep them only within the email thread.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: IP address, user agent string, the pages requested, referring URL and timestamps. These are strictly necessary for security and for serving the site, and are kept for 30 days.
  • Essential cookies and local storage: your cart contents, your session, and a record of the cookie choice you made in our consent banner. These are set without consent because the site cannot function without them, which is permitted under the relevant rules.
  • Analytics and advertising data: only if you consent. See sections 4 and 5.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or knowingly collect Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, financial account numbers, precise geolocation, biometric data, health data, or information about race, religion, sexual orientation, union membership or political opinion. If you volunteer any of that in an email to us, we do not record it in any system.

4. Cookies and tracking

A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device. We also use browser local storage, which works similarly. Our full inventory is in the cookie policy.

We place cookies in three categories:

  • Strictly necessary. Cart, session and your consent record. Always active. Without them the site cannot take an order.
  • Analytics. Aggregated measurement of which pages are used. Off until you turn them on.
  • Advertising. Advertising delivery, frequency capping and conversion measurement. Off until you turn them on.

Nothing in the analytics or advertising categories is set before you consent. The consent banner appears on your first visit with three choices: accept all, reject non-essential, or manage preferences. Rejecting costs you nothing; every part of the site works the same either way.

You can change your choice at any time using the cookie preferences link in the footer of every page, or by clearing cookies and local storage in your browser.

We do not currently respond to browser Do Not Track signals, because there is no agreed standard for what a website should do when it receives one. We do honour the Global Privacy Control signal, which we treat as a valid opt-out of sale and sharing under section 8.

5. Third parties, including Google

We use a small number of service providers. Each one receives only the data it needs.

Google

If you consent to analytics, we use Google Analytics to understand which pages are used and how people move through the site. If you consent to advertising, we may use Google Ads and Google AdSense to deliver and measure advertising.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and to other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info and the NAI opt-out page.
  • Third-party vendors and ad networks other than Google may also serve ads on this site. Where they do, they are listed in the cookie policy.
  • Google's own handling of data is described in the Google privacy and terms site.

Other providers

  • Payment processing. Card details go directly to our payment processor and never touch our servers. They act as an independent controller for fraud prevention.
  • Shipping carriers. USPS and UPS receive your name, address and telephone number in order to deliver the parcel.
  • Email delivery. Our transactional and marketing email provider receives your email address and the content of the message.
  • Hosting. Our hosting provider processes server logs on our behalf under a written data processing agreement.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months. See section 8 for what "sale" and "sharing" mean under California law, which is broader than the everyday meaning.

7. Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not direct any part of this site or our advertising to children, and we do not knowingly permit anyone under 13 to create an account or place an order.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email privacy@slowlinenorganic.com with enough detail to identify the record. We will delete it and confirm to you in writing, normally within five business days.

8. California residents: CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you specific rights. We honour these rights for all US residents, not only Californians, because operating two standards would be more confusing than it is worth.

Categories collected in the last 12 months

  • Identifiers: name, postal address, email address, telephone number, IP address.
  • Customer records: billing and shipping address, payment token, order history.
  • Commercial information: products purchased, considered or returned.
  • Internet activity: pages viewed, referring URL, and, with consent, analytics and advertising interaction.

We collect these directly from you, automatically from your device, and from our service providers listed in section 5. We use them for the purposes in section 3. We disclose them to the service providers in section 5 for business purposes only.

Do we sell or share personal information?

We do not sell personal information for money. However, under the CPRA, allowing an advertising cookie to send data to a third party for cross-context behavioural advertising can count as "sharing" and, in some readings, as a "sale". If you consent to advertising cookies, that activity may constitute sharing under California law. We would rather tell you that plainly than rely on a narrow definition.

You can opt out at any time and it takes effect immediately:

  • Use the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information preference control, which is linked in the footer of every page, and switch advertising off.
  • Or send a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser, which we treat as a valid opt-out request without requiring you to do anything else.
  • Or email privacy@slowlinenorganic.com with the subject line "Do not sell or share".

We do not sell or share the personal information of consumers we know to be under 16 years of age.

Your California rights

  • Right to know what we collect, use, disclose and share, including the specific pieces of information we hold about you.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold, subject to legal exceptions such as our seven-year tax retention obligation.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale and sharing, as described above.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect any, so there is nothing to limit.
  • Right to non-discrimination. Exercising any of these rights will not change the price you pay or the service you receive. There is no loyalty programme tied to data.

To exercise a right, email privacy@slowlinenorganic.com or call (828) 555-0142. We will verify your identity by matching the details you give against our records, and by sending a confirmation to the email address on the account. We respond within 45 days and may extend once by a further 45 days if the request is complex, telling you before the first period expires. An authorised agent may act for you with written permission that we can verify.

Residents of Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have broadly similar rights. Use the same contact details and we will apply the standard that gives you the most protection.

9. Note for visitors from the EU and UK

This is a US business selling only to US addresses, and we do not target the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. If you visit from there, the General Data Protection Regulation may still apply to that visit, and we handle it as follows.

  • Our legal bases are set out in the table in section 3.
  • You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that already happened.
  • Data is processed in the United States. Where a transfer from the EEA or UK occurs, we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses with the relevant processor.
  • You may complain to your national supervisory authority, or to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK.
  • We have not appointed an EU or UK representative, because our processing of EEA and UK data is occasional and limited to site visits rather than sales.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with a modern algorithm and are never stored in a readable form. Access to order data is limited to the staff who need it, protected by two-factor authentication. Card numbers never reach our systems.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable state breach notification law, without unreasonable delay.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change we will update the effective date at the top, post a notice on this page for at least 30 days, and email account holders. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy. Previous versions are available on request.
