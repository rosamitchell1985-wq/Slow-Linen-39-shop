Sizing and fit

Use the size guide. It lists the body measurement each size is drafted for and the finished garment measurement, both in inches, plus the US numeric equivalent of each letter size. Every product page also states the model's height and the size they are wearing.

If you are still unsure, email your chest, waist, hip and height to hello@slowlinenorganic.com and we will tell you what we would send. We do this several times a day and we are reasonably good at it.

Not as a separate range. Body lengths are drafted for a 5'8" to 6'0" frame. We hold a small number of long-body shirts cut 2.5 inches longer and can often ship one from stock at no extra charge if you ask before ordering. We hem trousers to your inseam free of charge at checkout, which covers most of the petite problem for bottoms.

Not meaningfully. Every woven fabric is washed twice before cutting and every knit is finished after knitting, so residual shrinkage is under one per cent. Buy the size that fits now, not the size you hope it becomes.

We use four fit names and apply them consistently: close is 2 to 3 inches of ease, regular is 4 to 6, relaxed is 7 to 9, and oversized is 10 or more. Ease is the difference between your body measurement and the finished garment. The size guide explains each one with the pieces it applies to.

Fabric and care

Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle, then hang it while still damp. That is the whole method. Do not tumble dry: heat and agitation together are what cause hard creasing and thinning at the folds. Do not use fabric softener, which coats the fibre and prevents the natural softening that happens with washing.

Linen is one of very few fibres that genuinely improves with laundering. A shirt at fifty washes is better than a shirt at five.

Because flax cellulose is highly crystalline and has almost no elastic recovery. The stiffness that makes linen last is the same property that makes it crease. Suppressing it requires a resin finish that costs 20 to 40 per cent of the fabric's strength, which we will not do. There is a full explanation in the journal.

Rarely, and cool. Wool resists odour well; airing a knit overnight handles most wear. When it does need washing, use a genuine wool cycle at 86°F or hand wash cool with a wool detergent. Roll it in a towel, press the water out, and dry flat. Never hang it wet and never tumble dry it, because heat plus agitation felts wool permanently.

Yes, gently. Undyed cellulose lightens slightly in sunlight over the first year or two and undyed wool warms a fraction. Because there is no dye, the change is even rather than patchy. Two pieces bought eighteen months apart will not match exactly, which is why we suggest buying pieces you intend to wear together in one order.

You can, and there is no reason to. Nothing we make requires it, dry cleaning solvents are harder on natural fibre than cold water, and it costs you money for a worse result. The only exception is a set-in oil stain on linen, where a specialist may do better than you will at home.

Orders and shipping

Orders over $150 ship free anywhere in the United States. Below $150 it is a flat $8, regardless of how many items are in the order. Standard delivery is three to six business days after dispatch; expedited two-day is $22 if you need it. Full detail is on the shipping page.

Not at the moment. We ship to all fifty states, Washington DC, Puerto Rico and US military APO and FPO addresses. International shipping is something we would like to do and we have no date for you, because customs handling at our volume is genuinely difficult to do well.

Within two business days for in-stock items. Made-to-order pieces take 18 to 24 business days. If an order contains both, we ship the in-stock part first at no extra cost rather than holding it.

If it has not shipped, yes. Email us with the order number as soon as you can and we will catch it if we can. Once a parcel is scanned by the carrier we cannot recall it, but you can refuse delivery or return it under the normal 30-day policy.

We collect sales tax where we are required to, which currently includes North Carolina and a number of other states under economic nexus rules. The exact amount is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and shown before you pay.

Returns and exchanges

Thirty days from delivery, worn or unworn. Exchanges and store credit ship back to us free. A refund to your original payment method has $7 deducted to cover the return label. We think it is fairer to charge the people who use the service than to raise everybody's prices to hide it.

Yes. You cannot tell whether a garment works for you without wearing it. Wash it first if you can, and send it back in any condition short of damaged. We launder returns and sell sound ones as seconds through our staff and a local clothing bank rather than destroying them.

Yes, on exactly the same terms. Nothing in our range is bespoke to your measurements, so a made-to-order piece is still a standard size and we can resell it.

Faults are not returns. Send a photograph to hello@slowlinenorganic.com and we cover postage both ways, at any age within five years for a seam, buttonhole or knitted join. The returns page has the process step by step.

Made to order and limited runs

Eighteen to twenty-four business days from payment, which is roughly four to five weeks including weekends. Two pieces are cut to order: the Ridge Line Utility Coat and the Raw Hem Linen Jean. We email you the week your piece goes on the cutting table so you are not guessing.

If we slip past 24 business days for any reason, we email you before the deadline rather than after, and you can cancel for a full refund at that point.

Because those two pieces sell in low enough volume that holding six sizes in stock would mean sitting on fabric for a year. Cutting to order lets us keep them in the range instead of dropping them, which we think is the better outcome even though it is slower for you.

Autumn Weave opens on October 9, 2026 and Winter Undyed on January 15, 2027. Both go live at 10am Eastern, first come first served, with quantities published that morning. There is no early access tier and no paid queue. The new arrivals page has the full schedule.

Gift cards

Digital only, from $50 to $500 in $25 steps. You choose the delivery date and we email the code to the recipient on that morning with a note if you write one. They never expire, they are not reduced by inactivity or dormancy fees, and any unused balance simply stays on the card for next time.

Gift cards themselves are not refundable for cash, which is standard and is also a legal requirement in some states. Anything bought with one is returnable on the normal 30-day terms, and the value goes back onto the card.

No. We looked at it and could not justify printing and posting a plastic or card object whose only function is to carry a code. If you want something to hand over, buy an accessory; every one is a single size.

Referral programme

Once you have placed an order you get a personal link in your account. A friend who uses it takes $25 off their first order over $150. Twenty-five dollars is credited to your account once their 30-day return window closes, so nobody is paid for a referral that came straight back.

There is no cap and no tier system. We do not post on your behalf, we do not ask for your contacts, and we do not email anybody you refer beyond the order confirmation they would get anyway.

Yes. Referral credit and gift card balance both apply at checkout and stack. The only restriction is the $150 minimum on the friend's first order, which is calculated before credit is applied.
