What we write about, and what we do not

The journal covers three things: how cloth behaves, how we make things, and how to live with the results. Every piece is written by somebody here who does the work, and every factual claim either has a source or is flagged as our own experience.

We do not write trend forecasts, we do not publish gift guides, and we do not run posts optimised for search that say nothing. If a subject does not need a thousand words, we answer it in the FAQ instead.

Who writes here

  • Marguerite Okonkwo founded the label and cuts most of the patterns.
  • Priya Raghunathan handles fabric sourcing and compliance.
  • Nell Fitzsimmons runs production and manages the shoot schedule.
  • Deshawn Ellery answers customer email and writes the care notes.
