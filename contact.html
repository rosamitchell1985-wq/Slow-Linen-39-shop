Visiting

Suite 200 is a working cutting and sewing room, not a shop, and there is no retail floor. We are glad to show people around if you email first and we can find an hour, but please do not turn up unannounced. There is no parking on Alder Mill Road itself.

If you want to try things on, one of our eleven stockists is the place. Alder and Ash on Coxe Avenue is a ten minute drive from us and carries the full range.

Before you write, these might be faster

Where is my order?

Tracking is emailed when the parcel leaves us, usually within two business days. If it has been longer than that and you have no email, check spam first, then write to us with your order number.

Shipping details

I need a different size

Start an exchange rather than a return. Exchange shipping back to us is free and we send the new size as soon as the label is scanned, not when the parcel arrives.

Returns and exchanges

Which size am I?

The size guide has body and garment measurements in inches for every category, plus the ease built into each fit. If it is still unclear, send us your measurements.
