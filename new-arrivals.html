The 2026 to 2027 release schedule

Limited runs happen when a mill has a small quantity of something good and no buyer for it. We take the whole lot, make what it yields, and that is the end of it. There is no restock and no waitlist beyond the email list.

Every date below is firm. Quantities are published on the morning a run opens.

How the notification actually works

Sign up in the footer of any page. We send one email the week before a run and one on the morning it opens. That is two emails per run, six a year at most. There is no early access tier, no paid queue and no bot protection theatre; stock goes live at 10am Eastern and it is first come, first served.

You can unsubscribe from any email and it takes effect immediately. Read what we do with your address in the privacy policy.

Why we do not run a spring and autumn collection.

A two-collection year forces a design decision every six months whether or not there is anything worth changing. It also creates the markdown cycle, because unsold spring stock has to clear before autumn arrives. Once you are discounting twice a year, you have to build the discount into the original price, and the customer who paid full price subsidised the one who waited.

Holding one capsule means we can price honestly, keep the same patterns in production for years, and get better at making the same thing rather than newer at making different things. The Weaver Band-Collar Shirt has been in the range since 2021 and is on its fourth pattern revision, each one a small correction rather than a redesign.

The limited runs give us somewhere to put curiosity without disrupting that.
