How to measure yourself

Use a soft tape. Measure over underwear or a thin layer, not over the clothes you are trying to replace. Keep the tape level and snug but not tight.

Upper body

  • Chest: around the fullest part, tape level under the arms
  • Natural waist: the narrowest point, usually an inch above the navel
  • Shoulder: from one shoulder point across the back to the other
  • Sleeve: from the shoulder point, over a slightly bent elbow, to the wrist bone
  • Neck: around the base of the neck, one finger under the tape

Lower body

  • Waist for trousers: where you actually wear the waistband, not the natural waist
  • Hip: around the fullest part, roughly 8 in below the waist
  • Inseam: from the crotch seam to where you want the hem, in the shoes you will wear
  • Rise: from the crotch seam up to the waistband, measured on trousers that fit
  • Thigh: around the fullest part of one leg

How much ease each fit has

Ease is the difference between your body measurement and the garment measurement. We name four fits and we use them consistently.

Close, 2 to 3 inches

The Undyed Merino Rib Sweater only. Follows the body without gripping. Take your usual size.

Regular, 4 to 6 inches

Most shirting, the Oat Crew Knit, the Long Linen Shift Dress. Room to move, no drape.

Relaxed, 7 to 9 inches

All outerwear, the Slow Wool Pullover. Layering room built into the pattern.

Oversized, 10 inches and up

The Weaver's Overshirt and the Field Smock Dress. Deliberately large. Size down for a normal fit.

If you are between sizes

Take the chest size for anything with sleeves and the waist size for trousers. Our shirting has a straight body with only a slight taper, so an M shirt will not pull at an L waist. The one exception is the Undyed Merino Rib Sweater, which is close through the body; take the larger size there.

Body lengths are drafted for a 5'8" to 6'0" frame. Above 6'2" a shirt will read short at the back when you sit. Email us before ordering: we hold a small number of long-body shirts cut 2.5 inches longer and can often ship one from stock at no extra cost.
