Shop the capsule

Campaign, August 2026

One afternoon, one wall, no styling budget.

We hung a painter's drop cloth on the side of the workroom and photographed the capsule between two and six in the afternoon. What you see is what daylight did to it.

  1. Home
  2. Lookbook

Why the campaign looks like this.

Fashion photography usually flatters cloth by lighting it hard from one side, which exaggerates the weave and hides the drape. It makes linen look crisper than linen is. We asked our photographer, Marguerite Oyelaran, to do the opposite: one bounce card, open shade, nothing to conceal a wrinkle.

Every garment in these frames came out of the same stock we ship. Nothing was pinned at the back, nothing was pressed between shots, and the models chose their own sizes from the size guide before they arrived. Two of the six are the people who cut and sew the range.

The point is not authenticity as a marketing position. It is that if the photograph shows you a garment behaving in a way it cannot actually behave, the photograph has cost you money.

The two-piece argument

An oat Everyday Linen Shirt over the Drawcord Linen Trouser in the same cloth. Worn as a set it reads as a suit; split up, both halves disappear into everything else in the capsule. This is the outfit we photograph most because it is the one people buy first.

Kwame is 6'1" and wearing M in both pieces. The trouser is unhemmed at 34 inches in this frame; we hem to your inseam free of charge if you ask at checkout.

  • Everyday Linen Shirt, $152, flax linen, XS to XXL
  • Drawcord Linen Trouser, $158, flax linen, XS to XXL

The dress that does not need an occasion

The Long Linen Shift is the piece customers write to us about most, usually to say they wore it somewhere it should not have worked. It has no waist seam, which is why it reads formal with sandals and completely ordinary with boots and the Fell Cable Cardigan over it.

Photographed here at the end of the afternoon, unpressed, after four hours of wear. The creasing across the lap is exactly what linen does and exactly what you should expect.

  • Long Linen Shift Dress, $215, undyed flax linen, XS to XXL
  • Adaeze is 5'9" and wearing S
