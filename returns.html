Thirty days, worn or unworn.

You cannot tell whether a garment works for you without wearing it, so we do not ask you to pretend otherwise. Send it back within thirty days of delivery in any condition short of damaged.

Who pays for what, in one place

  • Exchange for a different size or piece: we pay the return shipping. You pay nothing.
  • Return for store credit: we pay the return shipping. You pay nothing.
  • Refund to your original payment method: $7 is deducted from the refund to cover the label.
  • Faulty, damaged or wrong item: we pay both ways and there is no deduction, ever.
  • Original outbound shipping: refunded only if the whole order goes back, or if the item was faulty.

We charge for refund labels rather than raising prices to hide the cost. About nine per cent of orders come back, and two thirds of those are exchanges we absorb.

The process, step by step

  1. Email us within thirty days of delivery. Write to hello@slowlinenorganic.com with your order number, which item is coming back, and whether you want an exchange, store credit or a refund. You do not need a reason, though it helps us if you give one.
  2. We email a prepaid USPS label the same or next business day. Print it, or ask for a QR code if you would rather have the post office print it. There is no returns portal to log into and no form to fill in.
  3. Pack it in the mailer it arrived in if you still have it. Our kraft mailers have a second adhesive strip for exactly this. If it has gone, any bag or box is fine. You do not need to include the original packing slip.
  4. Drop it with USPS. Any post office, blue collection box that takes packages, or your regular carrier pickup.
  5. For exchanges, we ship your replacement as soon as the label is first scanned. We do not wait for the parcel to reach us. That usually saves four to six days.
  6. For refunds, we process within three business days of the parcel arriving. Your bank then takes its own time, typically three to five business days, occasionally up to ten for a credit card.

What we accept, and the three things we do not

We accept

  • Garments you have worn, washed, or both
  • Garments without their tags attached
  • Made-to-order pieces, on identical terms
  • Pieces we hemmed to your inseam free of charge
  • Items bought with a gift card or referral credit
  • Items bought as gifts, returned by the recipient without your order number

We do not accept

  • Anything past thirty days. Write to us anyway; we have made exceptions and we do not promise to.
  • Damaged goods. A tear, a burn or a set stain that was not there when it left us.
  • Gift cards. Not refundable for cash, which is also a legal requirement in several states.

That is the entire exclusion list. There is no restocking fee, no final-sale category and no seasonal exception, because there are no seasonal sales.

Faults and the five-year seam guarantee

A fault is not a return and it is not subject to the thirty-day window.

If a seam, a buttonhole or a knitted join fails within five years of purchase under normal wear, we repair it free or replace the garment if repair does not make sense. Email a photograph and your order number to hello@slowlinenorganic.com and we send a prepaid label. We pay postage both ways.

This does not cover accidental damage, moth holes, or a garment that has simply worn thin with age. Worn thin is what we want to happen; it means the piece did its job.

After five years we will still repair anything we made, charged at the cost of the labour. Typical figures are $15 to reset a button and rework the hole, $25 to patch an elbow, $40 to replace a pocket bag. Postage each way is yours. Email photographs first and we quote before you send anything.
