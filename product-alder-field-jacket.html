Alder Field Jacket

$268 USD. Free US shipping on this order.

The jacket the rest of the range was built around. Four patch pockets, a two-piece sleeve so it bends at the elbow rather than pulling at the shoulder, and no lining, no fusible and no shoulder pad anywhere in it. What holds its shape is the pattern and the weight of the cloth.

It arrives stiff. Two weeks of wear breaks the canvas at the elbow, the front edge and the shoulder, and after that it moves with you. Six months in it is the softest structured thing most people own.

Fabric

  • Composition: 55% hemp, 45% European flax linen
  • Weight: 11.5 oz per square yard, plain weave canvas
  • Mill: woven in Panevėžys, Lithuania, on rapier looms
  • Finish: washed twice before cutting, undyed, no softener or resin
  • Colour: natural oat. Batch to batch variation of about half a tone is normal

Fit

  • Cut: relaxed through the chest, straight at the hem, sits at high hip
  • Shoulder: set-in, two-piece sleeve, no padding
  • Room: takes the Oat Crew Knit underneath in your usual size
  • Pockets: four patch pockets, bar tacked at every corner
  • Closure: five corozo nut buttons, spare button sewn inside the placket

Care

  • Wash: machine wash cold on a gentle cycle, with like colours
  • Dry: hang dry on a wide hanger. Do not tumble dry
  • Press: warm iron on the reverse while slightly damp, or steam it
  • Do not: bleach, dry clean or use fabric softener, which coats the fibre
  • Repair: seam failure inside five years of normal wear is repaired or replaced free

Sizing

  • Range: XS to XXL. Roughly US 0 to 20 or a 34 to 48 inch chest
  • Model: Kwame is 6'1" with a 40 inch chest and wears M
  • Second model: Renske is 5'7" with a 36 inch chest and wears XS
  • Garment chest at M: 45 inches around, measured flat and doubled
  • Centre back length at M: 28.5 inches from the base of the collar

How this jacket is put together

Every long seam is flat felled, which means the raw edges are folded inside a second row of stitching. It uses more thread and takes about twice as long as an overlocked seam, and it is the reason a well-made work jacket outlasts a fashion one. There is nothing inside the jacket that can fray.

The armhole is set slightly higher than a modern jacket would be. That feels less roomy standing still and considerably better when you reach for something, which is the trade most contemporary patterns get backwards.

The buttons are corozo, the seed of a South American palm. They are harder than plastic, they take a knock without cracking, and they will eventually compost. A spare is sewn inside the placket because we would rather you kept the jacket than replaced it.

Straight answers

The cloth is washed twice before cutting, so residual shrinkage is under one per cent. In practice you will not notice it. Wash cold anyway; hot water is what damages linen.

On its own, down to about 55°F. Over the Slow Wool Pullover, down to about 40°F in dry weather. It is not a waterproof or an insulated coat and we would not sell it as one.

Undyed hemp-linen lightens slightly in the first year, more at the shoulders if you are often in the sun. It does not go patchy the way a dyed garment does, because there is no dye to lift.
