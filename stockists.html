Eleven shops, and why we are not in a twelfth yet.

Ninety-four per cent of what we make is sold on this site. The remaining six per cent goes to independent shops that we have visited, whose buyers we can name, and who order because they want the clothes rather than because a rep pushed them.

If you would like to try a piece on before buying, one of these is the place. Stock varies; call ahead if you are making a journey for a specific size.

Wholesale terms

We are open to a small number of new retail partners each year. Here are the terms in full before you write to us, so you can decide whether it is worth either of our time.

The commercial terms

  • Opening order: $2,500 at wholesale, minimum
  • Reorders: no minimum, shipped within five business days
  • Wholesale price: 50% of the US retail price shown on this site
  • Payment: net 30 after the first order, which is prepaid
  • Territory: non-exclusive, but we will not add a second shop within three miles

What we ask

  • Pricing: hold our published US retail price. No independent discounting
  • Channel: physical shop or your own website only. No marketplaces
  • Care information: pass on the care card with every sale
  • Returns: handle customer returns under your own policy; we take back faulty goods
  • Photography: use our images freely, credited or not, without alteration

What we will not do

We do not offer sale or return, we do not fund co-op marketing, and we do not do exclusives for a territory larger than a neighbourhood. We are eleven people; those arrangements need a bigger company than this one and pretending otherwise would waste your buying season.

We also do not sell to businesses that intend to resell on marketplace platforms, and we check.
